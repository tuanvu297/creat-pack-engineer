# Story, references and audience

## From brief to film
Write the audience's starting state, intended change, single thesis, verified claims and desired next action. Pick a story structure that makes the change legible: setup/conflict/mechanism/payoff is an option, not a mandatory formula. A hook should promise the actual payoff; shock or a question without resolution creates an expectation failure. Plan the first visible frame, onset of action and sound together.

Compress the script by removing repeated meaning, not by speeding every line. Each scene should add information or change emotion. Reserve time for the payoff and a clean ending. Choose duration from the brief, platform and story; old 24s/40s/120s ANA examples are not universal rules.

Build one content intelligence object: thesis, audience, verified facts, source evidence, angle, hook options, platform, format, policy risks, CTA and success metric. Adapt packaging without changing facts. Do not invent health outcomes, customer quotes, prices, facilities, logos or performance.

## Reference access
First record actual playable access, runtime, source URL/file ID, duration and audio/transcript availability. If the source is inaccessible, return SOURCE_ACCESS_PENDING. A URL, thumbnail or title cannot reveal its full editing, sound or physics.

Segment inspected media into functional beats: first-frame/hook, premise, escalation/reset, payoff, ending/CTA. At each timecode record action/framing, camera, text, sound, transition, narrative function and evidence label.

OBSERVED means directly seen/heard/measured. INFERRED means a plausible creative function supported by observations. HYPOTHESIS means a reason to test. Keep them distinct. Compare independent references and internal results before promoting a pattern. Views do not establish the causal effect of a hook or imply a ranking guarantee.

## Transfer without copying
Extract a rule such as "reveal the mechanism through an action" rather than a creator's exact script/sequence/graphics/audio/identity. Identify non-transferable protected or brand-specific expression. Translate patterns into the project's own canon, audience and source assets. Retain rights and factual accuracy independently of originality.

## Exercise output
Return a timeline card, three testable pattern candidates, source limits, an original shot recipe and what it must not copy. Grade actual access, timecode accuracy, evidence labels, source rights, coherent transfer and absence of fabricated claims. Reference analysis PASS is not a production-performance claim.

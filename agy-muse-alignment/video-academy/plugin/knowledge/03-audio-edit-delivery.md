# Audio, editing and delivery

## Voice
Treat provider language/voice labels as eligibility hints. Audition actual distinct voice IDs with the same passage; speed/pitch variations are not different speakers. Use dry voice first and comparable loudness. Listen for Vietnamese tones, names, consonants, natural stress, meaningful pauses, complete phrases and stable adult character identity. Do not imitate a named real presenter or silently substitute the locked speaker.

Record provider/model/voice ID, direction, input text, timing/settings and accepted sample. Changes in voice identity require the existing approval rule. An ASR transcript can detect missing/repeated words but not pleasantness, emotional expression or identity.

## Sound design
Map dialogue, room tone, ambience, foley, music and transitions to story beats. Avoid music masking consonants, abrupt ambience changes, clipped words or mechanical fades. Preserve silence when it serves intent. Check source licenses and platform use rights. Do not invent a universal loudness target; use the actual delivery spec and inspect peaks/loudness plus listening.

## Editing
Assemble accepted media only. Maintain screen direction, eyeline, action continuity, prop/light/wardrobe state and sound perspective. Cut on intention/action/information where motivated; effects are not a substitute for a coherent cut. Handle exact on-screen text in the editor when generated text is unreliable. Verify spelling, Vietnamese diacritics, timing, contrast and placement in the target aspect ratio.

For seamless joins, inspect the boundary in normal playback plus neighboring frames. Compare identity, position, scale, pose, light/color, motion vector and audio continuity. A dissolve can create ghosts/double images, a time remap can change physics, and noise reduction can damage speech. Recheck the repaired boundary and the full sequence.

## Export and handoff
Export a complete playable file to the approved staging destination. Record actual duration, streams, codec, dimensions, fps, size, hash and model/edit source IDs. Decode/check the file; a successful export dialog does not prove integrity. Watch/listen through the export, not just the editor preview.

Deliver source/edit project where available, final artifact, subtitles/voice/SFX provenance, receipt, QA findings and remaining limitations. Never publish as part of export unless separately authorized. Keep final acceptance destination distinct from working files and training evidence.
